## Verified candidate for an owner-approved replacement

A source with a documented origin is NASA Science's [Mars Planet Globe](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mars-planet-globe/), successfully retrieved through the web reader on 12 November 2026. The page identifies a Viking-orbiter mosaic, credits **NASA/JPL-Caltech**, and offers a 4,798 × 4,798 JPEG download. This is a candidate replacement with a known source, not evidence that the existing PNG comes from that page.

[NASA's Images and Media Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) returned HTTP 200 on the same date. They generally permit educational/informational use of NASA content with source acknowledgment, while distinguishing third-party copyrighted material and prohibiting implied NASA endorsement. NASA marks/logos have separate restrictions. Review the exact asset and current terms for the intended use before adoption; do not describe all NASA-hosted files as universally free of restrictions.

If the owner approves that candidate, record its exact download URL, download date, checksum, retained credit, crop/resize/transparency changes, and source/usage-guideline links. Suggested credit: **“Mars imagery: NASA/JPL-Caltech; Viking orbiter mosaic. Source: NASA Science, Mars Planet Globe.”** If modified, identify the modification. Do not imply that NASA reviewed or endorsed ARES.

A second option is an independently drawn geometric Mars illustration with its creator and creation history recorded. That avoids inventing a NASA origin but still requires a documented rights decision. Neither option has been substituted into the application.

Two guessed NASA resource slugs, `/resource/mars-full-globe/` and `/resource/mars-viking/`, returned HTTP 404; they are not valid replacement citations. The verified page above is a different URL.
